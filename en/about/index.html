About PAY ORBIT

PAY ORBIT PTY. LTD is an Australia-based technology company focused on enterprise networking, cybersecurity and digital infrastructure. We support organizations in building secure, scalable and resilient IT environments that can adapt to changing business and operational requirements.

What We Do

Our portfolio covers enterprise connectivity, data center networking, secure access, network security, cloud security and professional technology services. Rather than approaching projects as individual hardware purchases, PAY ORBIT aims to understand the customer's operating environment and recommend an integrated solution that is practical to deploy and manage.
